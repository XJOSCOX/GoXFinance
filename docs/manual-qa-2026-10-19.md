## Fictional arithmetic reconciliation

- Gross: `29 × $25 + 1.5 × $25 × 1.25 = $771.875`, rounded once to **$771.88**.
- Corrected actual net: `$771.88 − $81.88 = $690.00`.
- Partial bill: `$50 − $25 = $25` remaining.
- Utility: `$342.18 − $200 = $142.18`; second payment clears that remainder.
- Purchases: `$342.18` standalone utility payments + `$25` credit purchase = **$367.18**. Debt repayments are not another purchase.
- Cleared cash: `$1,000` direct debt payment + `$25` linked debt/bill payment + `$200 + $142.18` utility payments = **$1,367.18**. The linked payment counts once.
- Net recorded cash flow: `$690 − $1,367.18 = −$677.18`.
- Monthly obligations: `$300` variable utility estimate + `$50` linked auto bill = **$350**. The linked debt's $50 minimum is not added again.
- Pending `$50` and cancelled `$500` are excluded from cleared cash. The January $100 payment is outside October reporting. Voided $30 expense is excluded. Recorded debt balances remain $1,500 because no new principal allocation or creditor reconciliation was invented.
