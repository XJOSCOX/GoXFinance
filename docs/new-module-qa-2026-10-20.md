## Issue found and fixed

The settlement audit export read `afterJson.status` and `afterJson.amountCents`, but the saved audit payload wraps those values under `record`. This displayed Unknown despite correct stored terms and correct settlement totals. The exporter now reads that record wrapper, retaining its explicit financial-field whitelist and legacy unwrapped fallback. The new workflow asserts the exported accepted status and $500 amount. PDF pagination also keeps ordinary record blocks together when they fit on one page.
